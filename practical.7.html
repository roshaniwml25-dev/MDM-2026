Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for a course? +
Login to the student portal, select Course Registration, choose your subjects, and submit the registration form.
How can I check my timetable? +
You can view your class timetable by logging into the student portal and selecting the Timetable section.
How do I contact the department? +
You can visit the department office or contact the department through the official college email.
What if I forget my password? +
Click on the "Forgot Password" option on the login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.
